About Me

Hi! I am Jiajun Liang, an undergraduate student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Xudong Mao. I am an incoming PhD student at the Pattern Recognition Lab (PRLab), Nanjing University, where I will be advised by Prof. Chenyang Si.

My research centers on generative modeling for language and vision, particularly diffusion language models and unified multimodal understanding and generation. I also develop methods for image and video generation, with an emphasis on efficiency and controllability.

I welcome research collaborations and discussions. Please feel free to get in touch!
